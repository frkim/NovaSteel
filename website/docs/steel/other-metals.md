# How Other Metals Are Produced

Other metals are produced through different industrial processes depending on
their **ore** and **chemical properties**. In every case, the goal is the same:
separate the useful metal from the ore, remove impurities, and obtain a material
with predictable mechanical, chemical, or electrical properties.

## Aluminum

Aluminum is extracted from **bauxite**:

1. The bauxite is refined into **alumina**.
2. The alumina is then transformed into aluminum through an **electrolysis**
   process.

This requires a large amount of **electricity**, which is why aluminum
production is strongly linked to energy availability.

## Copper

Copper is generally produced by:

1. **Concentrating** copper ore
2. **Smelting** it at high temperature
3. **Refining** it to improve purity

## Other industrial metals

**Zinc, titanium, nickel,** and other industrial metals each require specific
extraction and refining methods suited to their ore and chemistry.

!!! info "A common goal"
    Whatever the metal, production aims to **separate the useful metal from the
    ore, remove impurities, and deliver predictable properties** — mechanical,
    chemical, or electrical.

## Summary

| Metal | Typical source | Key step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Aluminum** | Bauxite | Electrolysis of alumina (energy-intensive) |
| **Copper** | Copper ore | Concentration, smelting, refining |
| **Zinc, titanium, nickel…** | Various ores | Specific extraction and refining methods |
