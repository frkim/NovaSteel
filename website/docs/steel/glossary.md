# Glossary

Definitions of the key terms used throughout the Steel Knowledge section.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Alloy** | A material made by combining a metal with one or more other elements to improve its properties. |
| **Blast furnace** | A high-temperature furnace used to produce molten iron from iron ore, coke, and limestone. |
| **Basic oxygen furnace** | A converter that turns molten iron into steel by blowing oxygen into the metal to reduce carbon and impurities. |
| **Electric arc furnace** | A furnace that melts scrap steel or other metallic inputs using electric arcs. |
| **Slag** | A by-product that collects impurities during smelting or refining. |

## Other useful terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Coke** | A carbon-rich fuel made from coal, providing heat and the chemical reaction that removes oxygen from iron ore. |
| **Pig iron (hot metal)** | The molten iron produced by a blast furnace, containing too much carbon to be used as steel directly. |
| **Non-ferrous metal** | A metal that is not primarily based on iron, such as aluminum, copper, zinc, or titanium. |
| **Stainless steel** | A corrosion-resistant steel containing chromium. |
| **Cast iron** | An iron-based material with a higher carbon content than steel; hard but brittle. |
