# NovaSteel

## Engineering the future of steel

**NovaSteel** is a Luxembourg-based integrated steel producer operating blast
furnaces and rolling mills across four European countries. We combine more than
a century of metallurgical know-how with an **AI-powered production
optimization platform** to deliver high-quality steel responsibly, efficiently,
and sustainably.

## Who we are

We supply steel to demanding industries — automotive, construction, energy, and
advanced manufacturing — across Luxembourg, Germany, Belgium, and Spain. From
raw iron ore to finished rolled products, we control the full value chain while
continuously reducing our environmental footprint.
