# Markets We Serve

NovaSteel supplies certified steel to a diverse range of industries across
Europe. Each market has its own demands — and our integrated, AI-optimized
production is built to meet them.

## Automotive

The automotive industry needs steel that is **strong, light, and perfectly
consistent**. We supply cold-rolled and coated grades, including high-strength
steels that help manufacturers reduce vehicle weight while improving safety.

- High-strength and advanced high-strength steels
- Excellent surface quality for visible and structural parts
- Tight, repeatable tolerances backed by AI-stabilized processes

## Construction & infrastructure

From buildings to bridges and railways, construction relies on dependable
structural steel.

- Structural beams and sections
- Heavy plate
- Rails and reinforcing products

## Energy

The energy sector — from conventional power to renewables — requires durable,
high-performance steel for demanding environments.

- Plate and sections for equipment and structures
- Corrosion-resistant grades for harsh conditions

## Industrial & mechanical manufacturing

General manufacturing depends on a reliable supply of versatile steel.

- Hot- and cold-rolled products
- Bars, rods, and wire rod
- Custom grades for specific mechanical requirements
