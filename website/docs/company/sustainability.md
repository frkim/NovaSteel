# Sustainability

Steel is essential to modern life — and it can be made responsibly. NovaSteel is
committed to producing the materials society needs while sharply reducing the
energy and emissions required to make them.

## Our climate ambition

The steel industry is energy-intensive and carbon-intensive by nature. We treat
that as our biggest responsibility and our biggest opportunity. Our optimization
program is built to deliver measurable environmental gains:

- **−14%** energy consumption per ton of steel
- **−22%** CO₂ emissions
- **+8%** high-grade steel yield (less waste, more usable output)

## Levers for decarbonization

### Energy efficiency

Energy represents around **35%** of our total production cost. Our **energy
dispatch optimization agent** schedules energy-intensive processes around
electricity spot prices and grid conditions, cutting both cost and emissions
without compromising output.

### Recycling and the electric arc furnace route

Our **electric arc furnace (EAF)** operations melt recycled scrap steel into new
steel. Because steel is **100% recyclable** without loss of quality, the EAF
route is a cornerstone of a circular, lower-carbon steel economy.

### Predictive maintenance

A **physics-informed machine-learning model** predicts furnace lining
degradation up to **21 days** in advance. Preventing catastrophic failures —
each of which can cost around **€8M** — also avoids the energy, materials, and
emissions wasted by unplanned shutdowns and restarts.

### Process optimization and quality

By improving high-grade steel yield by **8%**, we produce more usable material
from the same raw materials and energy, reducing rework, scrap, and waste.

## Compliance and governance

Operating across the European Union, NovaSteel aligns with:

- **EU Emissions Trading System (ETS)** obligations
- **GDPR** for the protection of personal data
- The **EU AI Act** for trustworthy, transparent AI
- Sector-specific EU directives for industrial emissions and safety

## Preserving knowledge, protecting people

Sustainability is also about people. Our **generative-AI knowledge-capture
system** structures the expertise of retiring operators into searchable
procedure libraries, keeping safe and efficient practices alive for the next
generation of steelworkers.

## A pragmatic roadmap

We believe decarbonization must be **measurable and achievable**. Rather than
distant promises, NovaSteel focuses on concrete improvements — energy, yield,
reliability, and recycling — that compound year after year toward a cleaner
steel industry.
