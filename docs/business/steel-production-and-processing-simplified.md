# Steel Production & Processing — The 5-Minute Version

> A fast, plain-English summary of how steel is made and shaped. For the full
> story, read the [comprehensive guide](steel-production-and-processing.md).

## What is steel?

- **Steel = iron + a pinch of carbon** (under ~2%). That carbon turns soft iron
  into a strong, tough, versatile metal.
- Adding other elements (chromium, manganese, nickel…) and changing how the steel
  is heated/cooled creates thousands of **grades**, each for a specific job.
- Steel is the world's most-used metal — *and* one of the biggest industrial
  sources of CO₂ (≈7–9% of global emissions).

## The two ways to make steel

| Route | Starts from | Energy | CO₂ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Integrated (BF–BOF)** — NovaSteel's route | Iron ore + coke | Coke (coal) | High |
| **Electric (EAF)** | Steel scrap | Electricity | Lower |

## How steel is made — 4 steps

1. **Ironmaking — Blast furnace.** Iron ore, coke and limestone go in the top;
   hot air blasts in the bottom. The coke strips oxygen from the ore, leaving
   **liquid iron**. A wear-prone **refractory lining** protects the furnace; if
   it fails unexpectedly, it is catastrophic and costly.
2. **Steelmaking — BOF + ladle refining.** Pure oxygen is blown through the
   liquid iron to burn off excess carbon → **raw steel**. Then chemistry and
   temperature are fine-tuned to hit the exact **grade**.
3. **Casting.** Liquid steel is **continuously cast** and cut into semi-finished
   shapes: **slabs** (→ flat products) and **billets/blooms** (→ long products).
4. **Processing — Rolling & finishing.** Slabs/billets are squeezed between rolls
   like a giant pasta machine:
   - **Hot rolling** (steel glowing-soft) → big shape changes, coils & beams.
   - **Cold rolling** (room temperature) → thinner, stronger, smooth surface.
   - **Finishing**: annealing, pickling, coating (e.g. galvanizing), cutting.

```text
Iron ore ─▶ Blast furnace ─▶ Liquid iron ─▶ BOF + refining ─▶ Liquid steel
        ─▶ Continuous casting ─▶ Slabs/billets ─▶ Rolling ─▶ Finishing ─▶ Products
```

## What comes out

- **Flat products** (from slabs): sheet, plate, coil → cars, appliances, cans.
- **Long products** (from billets): beams, bars, rails, rods, wire → construction.

## Words to know

- **Blast furnace** — turns iron ore into liquid iron.
- **BOF / EAF** — furnaces that turn iron/scrap into steel.
- **Slag** — glassy waste that carries off impurities.
- **Grade** — a precise steel recipe + processing for set properties.
- **EU ETS** — puts a price on emitting CO₂.
