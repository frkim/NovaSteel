# About NovaSteel

NovaSteel is a Luxembourg-based integrated steel producer. We transform raw
materials into high-performance steel products for some of the most demanding
industries in Europe, while pioneering the use of artificial intelligence to
make steelmaking cleaner, safer, and more efficient.

## Our mission

To produce the steel that builds modern society — responsibly, intelligently,
and sustainably — by combining deep metallurgical expertise with advanced data
and AI capabilities.

## Our vision

A steel industry that is competitive **and** decarbonized, where digital
intelligence amplifies human expertise rather than replacing it.

## Our story

Founded on a long European tradition of iron and steelmaking, NovaSteel has
grown into an integrated producer operating blast furnaces and rolling mills
across four countries. Today, we are writing the next chapter: an
**AI-driven production optimization platform** that connects every furnace,
mill, and sensor into a single, continuously learning system.

## The NovaSteel difference

NovaSteel is built around an **AI infusion strategy** that targets the hardest
problems in steelmaking:

- A **physics-informed machine-learning model** predicts furnace lining
  degradation from thermal signatures, providing up to **21 days** of advance
  warning before a costly failure.
- An **energy dispatch optimization agent** schedules energy-intensive
  processes around electricity spot prices to lower cost and emissions.
- A **generative-AI knowledge-capture system** interviews experienced operators
  and structures their expertise into searchable procedure libraries, preserving
  know-how as skilled workers retire.

## Measurable impact

Our transformation program targets concrete, verifiable outcomes:

- **−14%** energy consumption per ton of steel
- **−22%** CO₂ emissions
- **+8%** high-grade steel yield
- **21-day** advance warning on furnace lining failures

## Responsible by design

Operating across the European Union, NovaSteel embeds compliance into every
process. We align with the **GDPR**, the **EU AI Act**, and sector-specific EU
directives, ensuring that our use of data and AI is transparent, fair, and
accountable.
