# Compliance

Operating across the European Union, NovaSteel embeds regulatory compliance into
every process — from how we collect and protect data to how we design, deploy,
and govern our AI systems. This page summarizes the main frameworks that shape
our responsible use of data and artificial intelligence, with links to the
official sources.

    This page is provided for general information only and does not constitute
    legal advice. Always refer to the official, up-to-date legal texts linked
    below for authoritative requirements.

## GDPR — General Data Protection Regulation

The **General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)** is the European
Union's data-protection law. It governs how organizations collect, store, and
process the **personal data** of individuals in the EU, and grants people rights
over their own data — including access, rectification, erasure, and portability.

At NovaSteel, GDPR shapes how we handle employee, operator, and partner data
across our platform. We apply principles of **data minimization**, **purpose
limitation**, and **privacy by design**, and we ensure that personal data used
to train or operate our AI systems is processed lawfully, transparently, and
securely.

**Learn more:**

- [Official GDPR text (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)
- [European Commission — Data protection in the EU](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection_en)
- [European Data Protection Board (EDPB)](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/)

## EU AI Act — Artificial Intelligence Act

The **EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)** is the world's first comprehensive
legal framework for artificial intelligence. It takes a **risk-based approach**,
classifying AI systems by their potential impact — from minimal risk to high
risk — and imposing stronger obligations (transparency, human oversight, data
governance, and robustness) on higher-risk systems.

NovaSteel's AI infusion strategy — predictive maintenance, energy-dispatch
optimization, and generative knowledge capture — is designed for **trustworthy,
transparent, and human-centered AI**. We keep operators in control, document how
our models are built and validated, and monitor them in production to ensure
they remain safe, fair, and accountable.

**Learn more:**

- [Official EU AI Act text (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)
- [European Commission — AI Act overview](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai)
- [EU AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/)

## Sector-specific EU Directives

Beyond data and AI, steelmaking is governed by a set of **sector-specific EU
directives and regulations** covering industrial emissions, energy, worker
safety, and carbon markets. Together they frame how a responsible steel producer
must operate in Europe.

| Framework | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Industrial Emissions Directive (IED)** | Permits and limits for pollutant emissions from large industrial installations. |
| **EU Emissions Trading System (ETS)** | The EU's carbon market, capping and pricing greenhouse-gas emissions from heavy industry. |
| **Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)** | Carbon pricing on imports of carbon-intensive goods such as steel. |
| **Occupational Safety & Health (OSH) framework** | Protection of workers' health and safety in industrial environments. |

These obligations reinforce NovaSteel's commitment to cleaner, safer, and more
efficient production — and our optimization platform helps us measure and meet
them.

**Learn more:**

- [Industrial Emissions Directive (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32010L0075)
- [EU Emissions Trading System (ETS)](https://climate.ec.europa.eu/eu-action/eu-emissions-trading-system-eu-ets_en)
- [Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM)](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism_en)
- [EU-OSHA — Occupational safety and health](https://osha.europa.eu/en)

## Responsible by design

Compliance at NovaSteel is not an afterthought — it is built into how we design
our processes and our technology. By aligning with the **GDPR**, the
**EU AI Act**, and sector-specific EU directives, we ensure that our use of data
and AI is **transparent, fair, and accountable**.
