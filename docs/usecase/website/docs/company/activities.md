# Our Activities

NovaSteel operates across the full steel value chain — from raw materials to
finished, customer-ready products. Our integrated model lets us control quality,
cost, and environmental performance at every step.

## Integrated steel production

We run both of the major modern steelmaking routes, allowing us to balance
output, flexibility, and sustainability:

- **Blast furnace / basic oxygen furnace (BF/BOF) route** — producing molten
  iron from iron ore, then converting it into high-quality steel.
- **Electric arc furnace (EAF) route** — melting recycled scrap steel to produce
  new steel with a lower carbon footprint.

To understand how these routes work in detail, see
[Making Iron & Steel](../steel/steel-production.md).

## From liquid steel to finished products

Once steel is produced and refined, NovaSteel shapes it into a wide range of
products through industrial forming processes:

- **Continuous casting** of slabs, blooms, and billets
- **Hot and cold rolling** into sheets, plates, coils, strips, and structural
  sections
- **Finishing operations** such as coating, slitting, and inspection

Learn more in [Shaping Metals](../steel/shaping.md).

## Research, data & AI

Beyond physical production, a growing share of NovaSteel's activity is digital.
Our data and AI teams build and operate the optimization platform that:

- Predicts equipment failures before they happen
- Optimizes energy use against real-time electricity prices
- Improves and stabilizes the quality of high-grade steel
- Captures and preserves the expertise of our most experienced operators

## Quality and certification

Our automotive, construction, and energy customers rely on consistent,
certified material. NovaSteel maintains rigorous quality management across every
process, from incoming raw materials to final dispatch.
