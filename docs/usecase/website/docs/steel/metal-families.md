# Understanding the Main Metal Families

**Iron** is a chemical element and one of the most important raw materials in
industry. In its pure form, it is relatively soft and not usually used alone for
heavy-duty applications.

**Steel** is not a pure metal: it is an **alloy** made mainly of iron with a
small amount of carbon. This carbon content gives steel much better strength,
hardness, and durability than pure iron.

## Iron-based materials

### Cast iron

Cast iron is also based on iron, but it contains **more carbon than steel**.
This makes it very hard and good for casting, but also **more brittle**.

### Stainless steel

Stainless steel is a special type of steel that contains **chromium**, which
forms a protective surface layer and helps prevent rust.

### Alloy steels

Other alloying elements — such as **nickel, manganese, molybdenum, or cobalt** —
can be added to create steels with specific properties.

!!! info "Carbon makes the difference"
    Moving from pure iron, to steel, to cast iron, the **carbon content
    increases**. More carbon generally means more hardness — but also more
    brittleness.

## Comparison of iron-based materials

| Material | Main composition | Key characteristic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Iron** | Iron (pure element) | Relatively soft; rarely used alone for heavy duty |
| **Steel** | Iron + small amount of carbon | Strong, hard, durable |
| **Cast iron** | Iron + higher carbon than steel | Very hard, good for casting, but brittle |
| **Stainless steel** | Steel + chromium | Corrosion-resistant |
| **Alloy steels** | Steel + nickel, manganese, molybdenum, cobalt… | Tailored, specific properties |

## Non-ferrous metals

Metals that are **not primarily based on iron** are called **non-ferrous
metals**. They are important because they offer properties that iron-based
materials cannot always provide.

- **Aluminum** — light and corrosion-resistant
- **Copper** — conducts electricity very well
- **Zinc** — often used to protect steel from corrosion
- **Titanium** — combines high strength with low weight
