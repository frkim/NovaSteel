# Case Study 12 - AI-Powered Steel Production Optimisation Platform

## Industry
Heavy Industry & Metals
## Headquarters
Luxembourg
## Operating Region
Luxembourg, Germany, Belgium, and Spain
## Regulatory Context
GDPR  - EU AI Act  - Sector-specific EU Directives

## Business Challenge
A Luxembourg-based integrated steel producer operating blast furnaces and rolling mills across four countries faces:
 - Energy costs representing 35% of total production cost with no real-time optimisation
 - CO₂ emissions under increasing pressure from EU Emissions Trading System (ETS) penalties
 - Furnace lining wear impossible to predict, causing catastrophic failures costing €8M per event
 - Quality consistency issues in high-grade steel for automotive customers
 - Skilled operators retiring faster than knowledge can be documented and transferred

## Transformation Objective
Implement an AI-driven production optimisation platform that reduces energy consumption, predicts equipment failures, improves steel quality, and captures operational expertise before critical knowledge is lost.

## Sample of Azure Services
	 - Azure IoT Hub
	 - Azure Digital Twins
	 - Azure OpenAI
	 - Power BI
	 - Microsoft Purview
	 - Azure Machine Learning
	 - Microsoft Fabric
	 - Azure Databricks
	 - Azure Arc

## Expected Outcomes
	 - Energy consumption per tonne reduced by 14%
	 - CO₂ emissions reduced by 22%
	 - Furnace lining failure prediction achieved with 21-day advance warning
	 - High-grade steel yield improved by 8%

# AI Infusion Point
A physics-informed ML model predicts furnace lining degradation based on thermal signatures. An energy dispatch optimisation agent dynamically schedules energy-intensive processes around electricity spot prices. A GenAI knowledge capture system interviews operators and structures expertise into searchable procedure libraries.
