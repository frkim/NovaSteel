# 14. 📌 Key Recommendations

*Audience: COO (1), CFO (19), Head of Manufacturing / VP Operations (2), Strategy
Director (20), steering committee.*

This section is the **recommendation-led close** of the analysis: what NovaSteel
should do, in what order, and where to place its bets.

---

## 14.0 The recommendation in one paragraph

> **Approve a time-boxed, single-line pilot** of the Microsoft **Fabric + Foundry**
> optimisation platform, ingesting telemetry **cloud-direct via Azure IoT Hub**, to
> prove the **21-day furnace warning**, **−14% energy**, **−22% CO₂** and **+8%
> yield** on real data behind a compliant **DPIA / EU AI Act** baseline. The business
> case is dominated by avoided **€8M** failures and a large energy cost base, giving
> **sub-12-month payback**, with a **G4 scale gate** before any four-country
> commitment. The risk is contained; the upside is structural; the technology is GA
> and EU-resident. **Proceed.**

## 14.1 Strategic priorities

1. **Attack the biggest lever first.** Energy is **35%** of cost — the dispatch agent
   (O1/O2) is the dominant value driver (~€24.5M illustrative). Make it the spine of
   the pilot.
2. **Buy reliability insurance.** A **21-day** furnace warning (O3) converts €8M
   emergencies into planned work — high-impact, board-legible, easy to back-test.
3. **Protect the premium franchise.** +8% high-grade yield (O4) defends automotive
   revenue via **SPC**, not automation.
4. **Make knowledge a durable asset.** Capture retiring expertise (O5) now — it
   compounds and underpins yield.
5. **Consolidate, don't sprawl.** One Fabric + Foundry plane and one governance
   fabric is the strategic platform choice.

## 14.2 Quick wins vs long-term bets

| Horizon | Move | Why |
|---------|------|-----|
| **Quick win (0–3 mo)** | Furnace **back-test** on historical failures | Proves 21-day warning with **existing** data, no plant risk |
| **Quick win (0–3 mo)** | Energy A/B vs. historical baseline | Demonstrates −14% potential on real series |
| **Quick win (3–6 mo)** | Knowledge assistant on synthetic + first real SOPs | Visible operator value; low risk |
| **Long-term bet (6–18 mo)** | Multi-site scale + CMMS/ERP integration | Repeatable operating model across 4 countries |
| **Long-term bet (12–24 mo)** | Carbon-aware dispatch at portfolio scale | Compounding ETS / CBAM advantage |
| **Future option** | Toward (supervised) closed-loop control | Re-assess AI Act high-risk obligations first |

## 14.3 Scaling strategy

- **Prove percentages on the pilot line (~0.3 Mt) before crediting at-scale (~1.0 Mt)** —
  benefits at scale are ≈3× the pilot.
- **Replicate, don't rebuild:** the single platform + IaC means each new line/site is
  a **configuration**, not a project.
- **Gate every step:** G1→G4 governance; **no big-bang cutover**.
- **Sequence by value & data readiness:** start where historian data is richest and
  energy spend highest.

> Against illustrative annual benefits where **energy alone is ~€24.5M**, this is a
> **sub-12-month payback** even under conservative haircuts. Fund it as a **strategic

## 14.5 What we are asking the steering committee to approve

1. **Pilot funding** for one furnace line, time-boxed, with the G1–G4 gates.
2. **A cross-functional team** and **historian/OT data access** under a DPIA.
3. **EU regions** (Sweden Central primary) and the security baseline.
4. **A scale decision gate (G4)** at pilot end, with refreshed TCO/ROI.

## 14.6 Decision summary

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Is the problem real and quantified? | Yes — energy 35%, €8M failures, yield, knowledge loss |
| Is the solution technically sound? | Yes — Fabric + Foundry, cloud-direct, physics-informed, agentic |
| Is it safe and compliant? | Yes — one-way OT, human-in-the-loop, EU-resident, AI Act mapped |
| Is the value compelling? | Yes — sub-12-month payback, energy lever dominates |
| Is the risk contained? | Yes — gated pilot, no control writes, conservative claims |
| **Recommendation** | **Proceed with the pilot.** |
