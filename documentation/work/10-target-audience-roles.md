# 10 — Target Audience Roles

## Session 1 — Strategic & Operational Decision‑Makers (1–10)

| Priority | Role | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | COO | Owns production efficiency, cost structure, downtime, yield, and cross-plant transformation. |
| 2 | Head of Manufacturing / VP Operations | Drives operational excellence across sites; directly accountable for KPIs. |
| 3 | Plant Director / Site Manager | Feels daily pain: furnace wear, energy peaks, quality drifts, operator shortages. |
| 4 | CTO / Head of IT/OT | Validates feasibility: SCADA, PLC, IoT, historian, edge compute, cybersecurity. |
| 5 | Head of Maintenance / Reliability Engineering Lead | Directly impacted by predictive maintenance & furnace lining forecasting. |
| 6 | Head of Quality | Owns high-grade steel consistency and automotive client requirements. |
| 7 | Head of Energy Management | Critical for energy dispatch optimization and ETS cost reduction. |
| 8 | CISO | Ensures industrial cybersecurity, OT segmentation, and AI system security. |
| 9 | Compliance Officer | AI Act classification, auditability, model governance, risk controls. |
| 10 | Data Protection Officer (DPO) | GDPR compliance for operator data, sensor data, and knowledge capture. |

## Session 2 — Governance, Technical, Workforce & Financial Roles (11–20)

| Priority | Role | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 11 | Head of Sustainability / ESG | CO₂ reduction, ETS penalties, sustainability reporting. |
| 12 | Chief Data Officer (CDO) | Data governance, data quality, cross-plant data strategy. |
| 13 | Head of Data Science / ML Lead | Validates ML models, physics-informed modeling, MLOps. |
| 14 | AI Architect / Digital Twin Architect | Designs architecture for predictive models and optimization agents. |
| 15 | OT Engineer / Automation Engineer | Ensures integration with PLCs, SCADA, historian systems. |
| 16 | Head of HR / Workforce Transformation | Critical for knowledge capture, operator training, and upskilling. |
| 17 | Union Representative / Worker Council | Important in EU industrial contexts for AI adoption acceptance. |
| 18 | Shift Supervisors / Senior Operators | Provide domain knowledge; key for GenAI knowledge capture. |
| 19 | CFO | Validates ROI: energy savings, downtime reduction, ETS penalties avoided. |
| 20 | Strategy Director | Ensures alignment with long-term industrial modernization roadmap. |

## Canonical naming guidance for all work documents

Use the role names above verbatim across `documentation/work/*.md`.

| Use this canonical role | Avoid legacy alias in this workspace |
| --- | --- |
| Head of Quality | Quality Officer |
| Head of Sustainability / ESG | CMO (for sustainability ownership) |
| Compliance Officer | Compliance Manager |
| Data Protection Officer (DPO) | Privacy Manager |

When a document needs a compact jury shorthand, keep the canonical role names
and optionally include priority numbers (for example, "Head of Quality (6)").
