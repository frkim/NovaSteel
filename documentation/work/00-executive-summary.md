# 00 — Executive Summary

**Project Ignition** — NovaSteel AI-Powered Steel Production Optimization Platform
*One-page brief for the executive jury (COO · CFO · Quality Officer · CMO · Compliance Manager)*

---

## The challenge

NovaSteel operates blast furnaces and rolling mills across four EU countries.
Four forces are eroding margin and resilience:

- **Energy** is **35%** of production cost, dispatched with no real-time
  optimization.
- **CO₂** is exposed to rising **EU ETS** penalties.
- **Furnace-lining failures** are unpredictable and cost **~€8M per event**.
- **Quality** of high-grade automotive steel is inconsistent, and **retiring
  operators** are taking decades of know-how with them.

## The solution

An **Azure AI platform** that ingests plant-floor telemetry and turns it into
three decisions:

1. **Predict** furnace-lining degradation from thermal signatures
   (physics-informed ML) — *21-day advance warning*.
2. **Optimize** energy-intensive processes around electricity spot prices and
   grid carbon — *lower energy & CO₂*.
3. **Capture** operator expertise with a GenAI assistant into a searchable
   procedure library — *protect & spread best-known methods*.

## The value (targets)

| Outcome | Target | Why it matters |
| ------- | ------ | -------------- |
| Energy per ton | **−14%** | Directly attacks 35% of cost |
| CO₂ emissions | **−22%** | Avoids EU ETS penalties; sustainability story |
| Furnace failure warning | **21 days** | Avoids ~€8M per averted catastrophic failure |
| High-grade yield | **+8%** | More saleable premium automotive steel |

> is proven.

## Why it is safe and compliant

- **EU data residency** throughout; personal data stays in the EU.
- **EU AI Act** risk assessment per workload; **human-in-the-loop** for any
  safety, emissions or personnel decision.
- **GDPR / DPIA**, **Microsoft Responsible AI**, and full auditability via
  Microsoft Purview, Entra ID and Defender for Cloud.
  See **[06 — Security & compliance](06-security-compliance.md)**.

## What we are asking for

- Approval to run a **time-boxed pilot** (one furnace line) to prove the 21-day
  warning and energy savings on real data.
- A cross-functional team and access to historian/OT data under a DPIA.
- A decision gate at the end of the pilot to scale across the four sites.

## The ask in each executive's language

- **COO** — fewer catastrophic failures, higher uptime, an operationally
  excellent, observable platform.
- **CFO** — a credible TCO with a payback driven by avoided €8M failures and
  −14% energy; conservative/base/optimistic scenarios.
- **Quality Officer** — tighter process capability (Cp/Cpk), +8% high-grade
  yield, full traceability — AI advises, metallurgists decide.
- **CMO** — a verifiable **−22% CO₂** sustainability and innovation story for
  automotive customers.
- **Compliance Manager** — GDPR + EU AI Act mapped to controls, EU residency,
  human oversight, and an audit trail.
